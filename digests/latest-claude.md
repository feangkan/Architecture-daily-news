## ⭐ Top 5

1. ★★★ **[AU 2026: Autodesk shifts AI from chatbots to agentic ecosystems](https://architosh.com/2026/10/au2026-autodesk-shifts-ai-from-chatbots-to-agentic-ecosystems/)** (Architosh, Oct 2026). See also [Illustrarch: "Autodesk confirms Forma will replace Revit"](https://illustrarch.com/architecture-news/115882-autodesk-confirms-forma-will-replace-revit.html) and [Autodesk's official AU 2026 roundup](https://www.autodesk.com/solutions/autodesk-university-2026-product-announcements).
   - Autodesk says that "over time" Forma will replace Revit, though not this year, and it will keep investing in Revit meanwhile. The AU announcements include AI documentation features (Autocomplete Room Elements, Optimized Annotations, Automated Documentation), a Sheets Agent (beta) and a standalone Assistant reportedly due in 2027.
   - _Why it matters:_ The dominant BIM vendor is moving to a cloud and agent workflow. Being fluent in Forma, its data model and its APIs now will look forward-looking on a CV, alongside Revit, which still runs most Australian practices.
2. ★★★ **[COP31: Türkiye tables an energy-efficient buildings framework (Antalya Compact)](https://energydigital.com/news/cop31-turkiye-tables-energy-efficient-buildings-framework)** (Energy Digital). See also [PBC Today](https://pbctoday.co.uk/news/planning-construction-news/cop31-turning-climate-ambition-into-action-for-the-built-environment/167109/).
   - COP31 runs in Antalya until 20 November. The presidency's Antalya Compact for Energy Efficient Buildings pairs emissions cuts with building resilience, and a ministerial meeting of the new Intergovernmental Council for Buildings and Climate was set for 12 November. GlobalABC says 163 countries now include buildings in their NDCs.
   - _Why it matters:_ Adaptation and resilience (passive design, urban greening, cooling) are getting policy weight. That framing is useful for research proposals and for sustainability narratives in your portfolio.
3. ★★★ **[Gensler Singapore: Regional Design Technology Lead – Computation](https://builtinsingapore.com/job/regional-design-technology-lead-computation/8043756)** (Built In Singapore listing)
   - The listing describes a junior-level, in-office role: building low-to-mid-fidelity computational tools for the region and training designers in computation and sustainability analysis. It asks for Power BI or data-visualisation skills and some machine learning experience. No posting date is shown, so check whether it is still open.
   - _Why it matters:_ It is a close match for a computational designer with an M.Arch, and it rewards tool-building plus teaching, both of which you can show with a small Grasshopper or Python tool and a short tutorial.
4. ★★☆ **[Walmart and retailers turn to Alquist for 3D-printed construction](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/walmart-3d-print-alquist-retailers/806899/)** (Facilities Dive, Nov 2026)
   - Alquist reports more than a dozen US retail construction projects using leased A1X printers (FMGI leasing, Hugg & Hall financing and service). Alquist presents this as 3D concrete printing moving beyond one-off pilots.
   - _Why it matters:_ Fabrication is starting to show up in commercial procurement models, not only in research labs. That gives a fabrication portfolio piece a credible "industry pathway" story. Note that the claims come from the vendor.
5. ★★☆ **[Winners revealed: 2026 National Architecture Awards](https://architectureau.com/articles/winners-revealed-2026-national-architecture-awards/)** (ArchitectureAU, 29 Oct 2026)
   - The Australian Institute of Architects' national winners were announced in Brisbane. Projects named in the coverage include Palmerston Street House (Robert Simeoni Architects), Merri Northcote (Six Degrees Architects) and 443 Queen Street (WOHA with Architectus and Kris Kowalski Architects). Check the full list in the article.
   - _Why it matters:_ It shows which Australian practices are doing award-level work right now, which makes a ready-made target list for applications and portfolio benchmarking (several of the practices are in Melbourne).

## 🧭 Evaluation

- **Credibility:** The Autodesk items rest on the official AU roundup plus trade press (Architosh, AEC Magazine, Engineering.com). Solid. The "Forma replaces Revit" quote is real, but the timeline is open-ended, so don't overstate it. COP31 coverage is mostly previews and advocacy (PBC Today, Energy Digital, GlobalABC) with no outcomes yet. Alquist/Walmart is framed by the vendor.
- **Hype vs substance:** "Agentic" is now Autodesk's word too, but the concrete features shipping are documentation automation. The full agent ecosystem is a roadmap (the standalone Assistant is reportedly 2027).
- **Gaps:** Searches for building-related research papers mostly returned *software* architecture papers on LLM agents, so no strong new building-research item today. No fresh (November) mass-timber, landscape or adaptive-reuse news surfaced. No Bangkok or Thailand architecture roles were found.
- **Job data caveat:** Many listings come from aggregators with no date. Treat them as leads and confirm on the employer's own site before applying.
- **Signal strength:** strong for tools and AI (Autodesk); medium for policy (COP31, in progress) and hiring; weak for sustainability, ecology and retrofit news today.
